# How to turn on two-factor authentication

To enable 2FA on your account:

1. Go to **Account Settings > Security**.
2. Select **Enable Two-Factor Authentication**.
3. Scan the displayed QR code with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator,
   Authy, or similar).
4. Enter the 6-digit code the app generates to confirm setup.

Once enabled, you'll be asked for a code from your authenticator app every
time you log in from a new device.

If you're having trouble with codes being rejected after setup, see the
separate article on troubleshooting rejected 2FA codes.
