# Password reset email never arrives

If clicking "forgot password" doesn't produce an email within a few minutes:

1. Check the spam/junk folder first. Reset emails are frequently filtered.
2. Confirm the email address on the account is spelled correctly.
3. Wait at least 10 minutes before requesting another reset. Requesting
   too many resets in a row temporarily blocks new emails for that account.

If none of this works, the account's email address may be unverified. An
unverified email can request a reset link but the email will silently fail
to send.
